Lightweight Bracket

A ribbed angle bracket concept for exploring how geometry affects stiffness and mass.

01CAD

Define the mounting faces and allowable envelope. Use rib spacing, wall thickness, and fillet radius as parameters so geometry can be compared across iterations.

02Engineering analysis

Define the load cases and mounting assumptions. Compare deflection and stress near the rib roots and attachment points against a solid baseline.

03Prototyping

Make an initial part to check fit, fastener clearance, and access for assembly. Record the material and manufacturing method used.

04Testing

Measure deflection at a repeatable load point and compare it with the analysis. Record the setup, load increments, and any permanent deformation.
