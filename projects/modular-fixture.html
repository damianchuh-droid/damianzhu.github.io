Illustrative case study

Modular Fixture

A concept for positioning different parts on a common base while keeping access for tools and measurements.

01CAD

Model the base, mounting slots, and removable stops as separate components. Check the range of part sizes and tool clearance in the assembly.

02Engineering analysis

Identify the forces applied by the clamps and check whether the stops can resist sliding and rotation. Review tolerance buildup between the slots, stops, and part.

03Prototyping

Build the base and one pair of stops. Check whether the hardware is easy to install and whether the stops can be repositioned without removing the base.

04Testing

Measure part position after repeated removal and reinstallation. Record the setup, measurement method, and spread of the readings.
